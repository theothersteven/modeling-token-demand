✨ Explore the model yourself!

Build your own comparative statics. Every point re-solves the user’s optimal scope s and effort x in your browser, using the same model and search bounds as the paper’s figures. Pick a regime, the variable to sweep on the x-axis, up to four outcomes to plot, and any number of industries with your own parameters. The paper’s eight figures are available as starting points.

Click a line label to toggle; double-click to isolate. Drag to zoom; double-click a chart to reset. The camera icon downloads a panel as PNG. Solutions are computed with a JavaScript port of the Python solver; the port is tested to agree with the paper’s audited results to relative error below 10⁻⁵.
